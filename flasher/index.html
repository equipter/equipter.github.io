Firmware flasher

Flash CYD firmware over USB. Choose a board profile for its firmware image, or use Custom for a full four-file flash. Files stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

This browser can't talk to serial devices. Use desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera.

1 Connect the board

Not connected.

If connecting fails, hold the BOOT button on the board while you press Connect, then release it once flashing starts.

2 Choose firmware files

Changing boards clears loaded files. Choose the matching board firmware.

Drop the selected board's firmware and any optional system .bin files here.
